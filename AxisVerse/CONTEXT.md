# AxisVerse Course Materials

AxisVerse students manage learning materials and use selected materials for questions and section summaries.

## Language

**Learning material**:
One student-visible document with its original filename and its related content for questions and summaries. Internal processing parts belong to the same learning material.

**Delete learning material**:
Permanent removal of the selected material's original file, extracted text, retrieval content, processing parts and related material records. Hiding an entry is not deletion.
_Avoid_: Hide, remove from list

**Remove from list**:
Reversible exclusion of a material from the student's visible list while its stored content remains available for restoration.
_Avoid_: Delete learning material
